# 🏥 MEDICARE – Smart Healthcare Management System

**MEDICARE** is a smart healthcare management platform designed to simplify healthcare services by connecting patients, doctors, and administrators through a single digital system.

It helps patients manage appointments, access medical records, track medicines, receive reminders, and monitor their healthcare activities in one place.

## ✨ Key Features

- 👤 **Patient Dashboard** – View health information and activities.
- 📅 **Appointment Management** – Book and manage doctor appointments.
- 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Management** – Explore doctors and their availability.
- 💊 **Medicine Tracker** – Manage medicines and dosage schedules.
- ⏰ **Smart Reminders** – Get reminders for medicines and appointments.
- 📋 **Digital Prescriptions** – Access prescriptions online.
- 🗂️ **Medical Records** – Maintain personal medical history.
- 🧪 **Lab Reports** – View and manage laboratory reports.
- ❤️ **Health Tracking** – Monitor important health information.
- 🤖 **AI Healthcare Assistant** – Get basic healthcare guidance.
- 🚨 **Emergency Support** – Quick access to emergency assistance.
- 📊 **Health Timeline** – Track healthcare activities over time.
